# Data and result boundary

This local candidate includes the reported capacity results and cross-source/source-level transfer aggregates used by the article and supplement. The original X04, X06, X07 and X10 analysis inputs and the three parity-reference copies are not included; their public redistribution permission has not been newly confirmed. The H2 publication tables comprise 64 cross-source summaries, 14 source comparisons at 50%, 32 source-policy-quota rows for the two sources outside the primary cohort and 24 training-selection records. Every reported source has at least 162 eligible establishments.

The 70 source-by-state count/event cells, the full 416-row private transfer table, original FAT microdata, establishment identifiers and row-level derivatives are excluded. Public source-specific files omit the five original-state contact shares, state contact/event masses, counts and full training objectives. The selection records report source labels, selected cuts and training-set sizes. Cross-source mean composition shares remain in the aggregate summary table; they do not identify source-state cells.

Two current public routes use the pinned original FAT archive without private input exports: `run_raw_pooled.py` reconstructs the original pooled results and deletion bounds, while `transfer/run_transfer_from_raw.py` reconstructs the source-state cells using the independent reader and then runs the unchanged exact transfer engine. It compares the resulting numbers with supplied publication aggregates. It needs no private parity-reference exports. Keep all newly generated private outputs outside a repository or public deposit. The World Bank FAT access terms are not replaced by any software licence or by an input hash. A local candidate does not assert a new repository release, deposited dataset, public DOI or redistribution permission for excluded data.
