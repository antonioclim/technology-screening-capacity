## Interpretation

Some synthetic fixtures fit models to artificial observations. No FAT microdata were accessed and no empirical models were re-estimated in these checks. A figure rerun reconstructs plots from reported tables; it does not reproduce the underlying empirical fits. Native Windows execution, a prospective validation sample and a new survey-design inference were not tested.

Historical run receipts retain their original dates and scopes in the private research archive. They are not counted as new tests of this revision. The two skipped extractor cases are not counted as passed tests.
