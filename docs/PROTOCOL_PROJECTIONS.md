## What is unchanged

The input data identities, sample rules, outcomes, feature definitions, fitting and evaluation weights, model specifications, allocation rules, selection procedure, reporting hierarchy and stated limitations are unchanged. A structural comparison restores only the listed publication-administration edits and verifies equality with every other field in the originals.

The four frozen E4 software copies remain byte-identical to their historical source. The numerical functions in the current runners are unchanged; their input-integrity constants identify the public protocol files.

## Historical receipts and ancestor references

Embedded ancestor digests in H4, its addendum, H5 and historical lock receipts continue to identify the original private protocol or code bytes. They have not been rewritten to present the public copies as an earlier freeze. A new local execution records the identities of its actual inputs and software. The historical status string retained by the E3 loader is an inherited schema discriminator, not a claim that the projected byte sequence was frozen at that earlier date.

The new protocol input digests can differ from retained historical run receipts even when the numerical definitions match. Compare the appropriate protocol identity or the documented projection mapping; do not assign old results to a new run.
