# Acquisition and constrained allocation

`software/run_h7.py` implements the H7 acquisition and allocation comparison. The inherited H4/H5 modules provide dependencies; they are not separate H7 command-line entry points. The public protocol projection and identity bindings are documented in [Protocol projections](../docs/PROTOCOL_PROJECTIONS.md).

From the repository root, use `reproduce.py --mode acquisition` with the separately obtained original FAT archive and a new output directory outside the repository. The mode runs all 268 prescribed model fits, nested selection and every retained policy, quota and source. Individual records and prediction/allocation vectors remain in memory; generated source/group/fit outputs are private analytical results and are not part of the distributed reporting tables.

The scientific specification preserves the source-relative event labels, expected survey-weighted contact contract and adverse results. Addenda A01/A02 were fixed before the first H7 fitting. R01 corrected a descriptive numerical sign count after that run without changing any scientific policy or numerical result. The analysis follows earlier data exposure and does not establish prospective validation, causal programme benefits or observed information costs.

`independent/independent_h7.py` supplies a separate allocation and inverse calculation. Its synthetic, empirical and comparison commands are documented in [Independent checks](../INDEPENDENT_CHECKS_EN.md).
